## Problem Statement

Kuku releases new AI-generated vertical microdrama series every week (each 50+ episodes, ~1 to 3 minutes per episode). Today, a human promo editor watches every episode by hand (~103 minutes of footage per 50-episode show) to find 20 to 60-second promotional clips for Instagram Reels, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts.

Our goal is to build a repeatable, show-agnostic, model-swappable **Microdrama Promo Finder Studio** that reads timestamped episode screenplays (starting with the 50-episode input pack for *I Married a Broken Soldier. He was a Duke*), extracts both **single continuous promo stretches (20.0s to 60.0s)** and **multi-moment stitched promos (2 to 3 segments totaling 20.0s to 60.0s)**, ranks them in **"Post First" campaign acquisition order**, exports **1-click FFmpeg/CSV editor cut-sheets**, and exposes an empirical **Quality & Failure Audit** showing where automated selection and naive LLM prompting fail.

## Demand Evidence & Dataset Reality

Direct inspection of `/home/venkat/projects/kuku/I Married a Broken Soldier. He was a Duke-scripts (2).zip` confirms:
- **Volume**: 50 `.txt` screenplay files (`episode-001-Episode 1671703.txt` through `episode-050-Episode 1686810.txt`), 100 scenes total, **3,727 total cues**, totaling **6,165.0 seconds (102.8 minutes)** of runtime (`min: 70.5s`, `max: 200.75s`, `mean: 123.3s`).
- **Multi-Track Overlapping Timelines**: Cues are tagged `**CAMERA:**`, `**ACTION:**`, `**SOUND FX:**` (`114` occurrences), or `**CHARACTER (Emotion):**` with `Start: (MM:SS.CC) | End: (MM:SS.CC)` timestamps. Across the 50 files, **2,392 cue pairs overlap in time** because camera cuts, actions, SFX, and spoken dialogue run on parallel tracks.
- **Explicit Cliffhanger Markers**: 22 of the 50 episodes explicitly contain the word `"cliffhanger"` inside their final `**SOUND FX:**` or `**ACTION:**` cues (e.g., Ep 1 `02:15.00`: *"Solemn low-pitched string drone swells to an ominous cliffhanger peak"*).

## Status Quo & Why Naive LLM Wrappers Fail

1. **Manual Status Quo**: Watching 103 minutes of footage and logging timestamps takes 3 to 4 hours per show per editor.
2. **Naive LLM Failure Mode #1 ("The Timestamp Hallucination & Mid-Sentence Cut")**: Feeding raw screenplay text into an LLM regularly produces invalid durations outside 20-60s or slices dialogue mid-sentence by snapping to an overlapping `**CAMERA:**` cue.
3. **Naive LLM Failure Mode #2 ("The Payoff Trap")**: Standard LLMs gravitate toward emotional resolutions (where the protagonist wins or the secret is answered). In short-form ad ops, giving away the payoff destroys click-through rate (CTR). A high-converting promo must cut **before** the resolution ("The Withheld Payoff").
4. **Naive LLM Failure Mode #3 ("The Lore Trap")**: Late-season episodes (`episode-030` witchcraft conspiracy, `episode-048` palace politics) contain intense dialogue that scores high on sentiment, but fails Kuku's requirement that a promo *"makes sense on its own to someone who has never seen the show"* because a cold viewer does not know the named characters or prior plot.

## Target User & Accepted Scope (Baseline + Selective Expansion)

- **Target User**: Growth / Promo Editor at Kuku (and the Kuku interviewer evaluating this tool live on a brand-new screenplay input pack).
- **Accepted Scope**:
  1. **Multi-Format, Multi-Track Screenplay Parser (`src/parser.py`)**: Parses `.zip` or `.txt` scripts, handles `(MM:SS.CC)`, `MM:SS`, `HH:MM:SS`, and `[MM:SS - MM:SS]` formats, normalizes scene-reset clocks if Scene 2 restarts at `00:00`, separates the **Dialogue Track** from **Camera/Action/SFX Tracks**, and dynamically builds the show's character proper-noun dictionary from speaker tags.
  2. **Dialogue-Safe Window & Stitch Engine (`src/candidates.py`)**: Generates valid `20.0s to 60.0s` windows whose `start_sec` and `end_sec` never slice an active `[d_start, d_end]` dialogue interval; applies Non-Maximum Suppression (`IoU >= 0.45`); extracts `withheld_payoff_preview` from the subsequent cues or **Episode `N+1`'s opening cues** when cutting at an episode cliffhanger; and builds multi-segment stitches using two ReelShort templates (**Intra-Episode Ellipsis** and **Cold-Open Flash-Forward**).
  3. **Pluggable 5-Call Macro-Batched LLM Judge (`src/llm_judge.py`)**: Supports `gemini`, `openai`, `anthropic`, `cli`, and `heuristic` providers; batches 10 episode candidate sets per prompt (5 calls for 50 episodes) with SHA-256 disk caching (`.cache/llm_cache.json`) and 3-layer error rescue (JSON fence stripper, fictional-drama framing, and per-candidate Stage-1 fallback with visible badge).
  4. **Live Model-Swap Diff & Naive-LLM Failure Benchmark (`src/benchmark.py`)**: Side-by-side comparison of any two providers/models, plus an empirical benchmark comparing a Naive Single-Prompt LLM against our Two-Stage Hybrid Funnel (`raw_outputs/where_ai_got_it_wrong.md`).
  5. **Interactive Promo Studio (`app.py`) & CLI (`cli.py`)**: Features the **50-Episode Series Tension Heatmap**, **Timeline Cut & Withheld Payoff Inspector**, **Scale & Unit-Economics Calculator (1 to 200 shows/week)**, **Failure Audit Tab**, and **1-Click FFmpeg / CSV Editor Cut-Sheet Exporter** (`raw_outputs/editor_cut_sheet.csv`).

## Explicit Assumptions (Where the Brief is Silent)

1. **Duration Constraint on Multi-Segment Promos**: The sum of segment durations in a stitched promo must fall strictly within `20.0s <= total_duration <= 60.0s`, with each individual segment lasting at least `6.0s`.
2. **Dialogue-Safe Cut Alignment**: Start and end timestamps snap to cue boundaries that do **not** intersect any active spoken dialogue interval `[d_start, d_end]` on the Dialogue Track.
3. **Hook Window Definition**: The "first 3 seconds" hook is evaluated on all cues overlapping `[window_start, window_start + 3.5s]`.
4. **Cross-Episode Cliffhanger Verification**: When `end_sec` lands within 5 seconds of `scene_end` / episode end, `withheld_payoff_preview` is pulled from the next scene or the opening 6 seconds (and `CONTINUITY NOTES`) of **Episode `N+1`**.
5. **Show-Agnostic Spoken Lore Detection**: Proper-noun lore density is measured strictly on **spoken dialogue lines** (ignoring `**CAMERA:**`/`**ACTION:**` production names) using character names dynamically extracted from the show's speaker headers.

- **Database / multi-tenant auth server**: Unnecessary overhead for a single-user local/hosted prototype and live interview test.

### 2. "What already exists"
- `/home/venkat/projects/kuku/I Married a Broken Soldier. He was a Duke-scripts (2).zip`: 50 episodes, 100 scenes, 3,727 cues (read directly in-memory via `zipfile` while `.gitignore` protects Kuku's raw IP from public repo pushes).
- Local runtimes (`python3`, `uv`, `gemini` CLI, `claude` CLI) and rendered UI sketch (`docs/designs/promo-studio-sketch.png`).

### 3. "Dream state delta"
- This plan delivers the full screenplay-level autonomous promo engine, multi-segment stitcher, failure auditor, and FFmpeg cut-sheet exporter, leaving only raw MP4 computer-vision frame alignment for a future Phase 2 production integration.
